Privacy Policy

Smash Fall · Codoxa Studio · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Codoxa Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game Smash Fall (the "Game"). We keep it short because the Game collects very little.

1. Who we are

Smash Fall is published by Codoxa Studio. You can contact us at codoxastudio@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The Game stores the following only on your device: your level progress, coins, number of lives, and your sound, music and vibration settings. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and shows advertising. You choose when to watch a rewarded video, for example to get an extra life, extra balls, or a double reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your device),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • information about how you interact with ads.

We do not receive this data about you as an individual. How Google uses it is described in Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the Game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.
  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you do not consent to personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

5. Children

Smash Fall is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party involved with data in the Game is the advertising provider described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game. Data held by our advertising provider is handled under its own policy.

8. Security

We keep the information the Game handles to a minimum, which reduces risk. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are handled by deleting the Game's local data as described above or through your device and Google account privacy settings. You can also email us at codoxastudio@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new features or services. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email codoxastudio@gmail.com.
